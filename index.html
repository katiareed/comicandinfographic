Panel Layouts and Add-Ons

L1

Three-Panel Strip

Best for: quick recaps, slides, exit tickets

Layout: one horizontal row of 3 equal panels showing setup, development and payoff. Add a title bar above the strip reading [TITLE] and a source line below it: Based on [PDF TITLE]. Leave even white gutters between panels. Format: [16:9 OR A4 LANDSCAPE].

Best for: Instagram, phone screens, short gags

Best for: full stories, summaries, class comic books

Layout: a grid of 6 equal panels, 2 across and 3 down, read left to right and top to bottom. Make panel 1 an establishing scene and panel 6 a summary of the key learning from the PDF. Format: [A4 PORTRAIT].

Best for: posters, displays, chapter openers

Layout: one large full-page splash image of [KEY SCENE] with [NUMBER] small inset panels overlapping the edges, each zooming in on a detail or fact from the PDF. Add a bold comic title at the top and a caption box at the bottom. Format: [POSTER SIZE].

Power Add-Ons

Paste any line at the end of a prompt to sharpen the result.

  • AKeep the same character design, outfit and colours in every panel.
  • BUse only facts from the attached PDF and do not invent information.
  • CMake all lettering large, legible and spelled correctly.
  • DUse original characters only, never real people or existing characters.
  • EEnd with a panel of 3 key takeaways and the PDF title as the source.

Responsible Use

  1. 1If the text comes out garbled, generate one panel at a time.
  2. 2Check every fact and quote against the PDF.
  3. 3Let students write or edit the dialogue themselves.
  4. 4Credit the source and label the comic as AI-assisted.
